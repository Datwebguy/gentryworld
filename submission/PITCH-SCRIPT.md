# GentrY — founder pitch video

**Target length:** 1:40–1:55. Speak naturally; replace the two bracketed details before recording.

> Hi, I’m **[your name]**, joining you from **[your city and country]**. I’m building GentrY for the Metropolis hackathon on Monad.
>
> Independent traders are being asked to make decisions across disconnected tools. Market data sits in one place, research in another, and agents often give answers without making it clear what information they used. Then the trader still has to move to a separate platform to act.
>
> GentrY connects those steps in one workspace for independent traders and the people building financial agents. You can follow market movement, ask a research agent about a market, inspect its registered identity and service price, and prepare a trade with the details in front of you.
>
> Here is how we keep the answer grounded. GentrY reads the current public market context from Perpl on Monad Testnet and gives that snapshot to the research model along with your question. The model explains the supplied data; it does not invent the quote or place an order. You can see the source and time, then decide what to do.
>
> If you choose to trade, GentrY shows the market, direction, size, leverage and estimated costs. You review them and explicitly confirm before the order is sent through Perpl. When there is a fill, we verify its transaction receipt on Monad and link directly to the explorer.
>
> Agent identities and reputation use ERC-8004, and paid agent requests use x402 so the price is clear and the wallet approves the payment. That gives agent builders a way to offer useful services and gives traders a way to evaluate them before paying.
>
> We built GentrY because financial agents should be useful in a real decision workflow: grounded in current information, easy to inspect, and under the user's control. Our first users are independent traders exploring Monad and agent builders who want their services to be discoverable and accountable.
>
> This is GentrY: market context, agent services and user-approved execution, connected on Monad.
